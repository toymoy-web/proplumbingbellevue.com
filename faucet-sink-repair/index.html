Faucet & Sink Repair in Bellevue, WA

Leaking faucets, failed shutoff valves, and sink fixture replacements, most handled in a single visit.

Typical VisitSame-Day

A dripping faucet is usually a worn cartridge or washer, and it's one of the more straightforward repairs we do, but a shutoff valve that won't fully close is a different problem that's easy to miss until it fails completely. We check both when we're already under the sink.

In older Bellevue homes, original shutoff valves from the 1960s and 70s are a common find, and many have never been operated since installation. A valve like that can seize or fail to seal the first time it's actually turned, which is exactly the wrong moment to discover it. We replace these proactively when we're already doing a faucet repair, rather than leaving a homeowner with a valve that might not hold next time it's needed.

Kitchen and bathroom faucets wear differently depending on use, and a pull-down kitchen faucet's internal cartridge typically fails sooner than a standard bathroom faucet given how much more it's used day to day. When we're repairing one fixture, we'll mention if a sibling fixture in the same bathroom or kitchen looks close to needing the same repair, so you're not caught off guard by a second call a few months later.

When we replace a kitchen faucet, we also check the sink's mounting and caulking while we're there. A worn seal around an undermount sink is a slow, easy-to-miss source of water damage to the cabinet below, unrelated to the faucet itself but often discovered during the same visit. Catching it early means one visit instead of a second call once the cabinet damage becomes obvious.

What Affects The Price

Faucet and sink repair pricing depends on whether it's a repair or a full fixture replacement, and whether the shutoff valve needs replacing at the same time.

Repair vs. replacement

A cartridge or washer swap costs less than replacing the whole faucet body.

Fixture brand and age

Some older or discontinued faucets need a full replacement since matching parts aren't available.

Shutoff valve condition

A seized or failing valve found during the repair adds to the visit but prevents a future emergency.

Under-sink access

A cramped or blocked cabinet takes longer to work in than clear access.
