Common InLower-Elevation Homes

Bellevue properties near Lake Washington and Meydenbauer Bay, and some lower-lying inland lots, deal with a higher water table than the rest of the city, which makes a functioning sump pump a real necessity rather than a nice-to-have. We install new systems and repair or replace units that have failed.

A pump that runs constantly, one that never seems to run at all, or a backup battery system that's never been tested are the most common issues we find. We test the full system, not just the pump itself, including the check valve and discharge line, since a failure anywhere in that chain causes the same result: water where it shouldn't be.

A sump pump installation isn't just the pump itself. The discharge line needs to route water far enough from the foundation that it doesn't cycle back in, and in some Bellevue properties near the lake, that routing needs to account for property line and drainage easement rules. We handle that planning as part of a new installation rather than treating the pump as a standalone part.

For homes with a finished basement, we also discuss a water alarm as a low-cost addition alongside the pump itself. It gives an early warning if the pump fails between routine checks, rather than the first sign of trouble being water already on the floor. For a finished space with real furniture and flooring at risk, it's usually worth the modest extra cost.

Emergency Response

Emergency Sump Pump Failure And Basement Flooding

A sump pump that fails during heavy rain can flood a basement or crawlspace quickly. If the pit is full and the pump is silent, running constantly, or humming without moving water, the system is not keeping up.

Stay out of standing water near outlets, shut off power to the area if it is safe to do so, and call our emergency line. We restore pumping, and if water is already in the home we point you to water damage restoration.

When A Basement Or Crawlspace Takes On Water

This usually comes up after a homeowner notices water in a basement or crawlspace during heavy rain, or after an existing sump pump fails and they find out only when water is already accumulating. A sump pump manages groundwater before it becomes standing water inside the home, and it's worth having a working one in any Bellevue property where this has happened before, or where the water table sits close to the surface.

Local Neighborhoods

Wet Ground Near The Lake, The Slough, And The Creeks

Groundwater is a real issue near Lake Washington, Mercer Slough, Kelsey Creek, and Phantom Lake. Homes in Enatai, Newport Shores, and the Meydenbauer Bay area sit close to the lake, and Lake Hills homes sit near Larsen Lake and the greenbelt. A sump pump in these areas works hardest from fall through spring, so testing it before the wet season is the best time to find a weak float switch.

Pump Types

Sump Pump And Ejector Pump Failures

Sump pumps and ejector pumps fail for different reasons. A sump pump handles groundwater, and a stuck float, a clogged intake or a failed switch is common. An ejector pump lifts wastewater from a basement bathroom, and a failure shows up as slow drains or a bad smell.

We test the pump, float and check valve, replace what has failed, and tell you if the pit or the discharge line is undersized. Bellevue's sewer standards bar storm drainage connections to the sanitary sewer unless the utility approves them, so we plan the discharge with that in mind.
