Emergency Toilet Repair & Installation in Bellevue, WA

Running toilets, clogs, and full replacements, most handled in a single visit.

Typical VisitSame-Day

A running toilet is one of the most common calls we get, and it's usually a worn flapper or a fill valve that's not seating correctly, both inexpensive fixes. A toilet that clogs repeatedly, on the other hand, sometimes points to a partial blockage further down the line rather than the toilet itself.

We also handle full replacements, whether that's an older toilet that's cracked or failing repeatedly, or a homeowner upgrading to a more water-efficient model. Installation includes checking the wax ring and shutoff valve, both common points of a leak if they're not properly seated. We check both before we consider the job finished.

Original toilets in Bellevue homes built before the mid-1990s often use significantly more water per flush than current models, and beyond the efficiency difference, these older fixtures are also more prone to the internal components simply wearing out from decades of use. When we're called for a repair on a toilet already at that age, we'll tell you honestly whether a repair makes sense or whether replacement is the more practical long-term choice.

For a bathroom remodel or a full renovation, toilet placement sometimes needs to shift to accommodate a new layout, which means moving the drain connection itself rather than just swapping the fixture in place. This is a bigger job than a standard installation, since it involves working with the floor's plumbing rough-in, and it's worth planning for early in a remodel rather than discovering mid-project that the original fixture location was assumed to be fixed.

Emergency Response

Emergency Toilet Repair For Overflows And Clogs

An overflowing toilet or one that will not stop running can flood a bathroom in minutes. Turn the shutoff valve behind the toilet clockwise to stop the water, and lift the float or flapper chain if the tank is still filling.

If water is also rising in a tub or shower, the problem is the main line and not the toilet. Stop using water and call our emergency line, and we clear the line and repair the fixture.

Running And Leaking

Running Toilets And Leaks At The Base

A toilet that runs on its own or leaks at the base wastes water and can damage the floor. The usual causes are a worn flapper, a failing fill valve, or a wax ring that has lost its seal.

All three are quick repairs, and catching a leak at the base early protects the subfloor from slow water damage.

Clogs

Toilet Clogs And Backups

A clog a plunger cannot clear, or a toilet that backs up when a different fixture drains, points past the bowl. We clear the clog and, if it keeps returning, check the line behind it.
