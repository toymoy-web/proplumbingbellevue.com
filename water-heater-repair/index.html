No hot water and a leaking tank are the two calls we get most on this. They're different problems: no hot water is usually an electrical, gas, or thermostat issue that doesn't necessarily mean the tank itself has failed, while a leak from the tank body, rather than a fitting or valve, usually does mean the unit is done.

We diagnose which one it actually is before recommending anything. A failed thermocouple, a tripped high-limit switch, or a bad heating element are all repairable without replacing the whole unit, and we'd rather fix the actual problem than sell a replacement a repair would have solved. All repairs meet Uniform Plumbing Code and manufacturer safety standards.

Gas and electric units fail differently, and we diagnose accordingly. A gas unit with no hot water often traces to a failed thermocouple or a pilot that won't stay lit; an electric unit more often points to a failed heating element or a tripped high-limit switch. Knowing which system you have before we arrive, even just tank versus tankless and gas versus electric, helps us bring the right parts on the first visit.

We also check the anode rod during a repair visit when the tank is otherwise sound, since a depleted anode rod is one of the more overlooked reasons a tank corrodes from the inside faster than it should, especially in homes with harder water. Replacing it during an unrelated repair visit is a small addition to the job but can meaningfully extend how long the existing tank lasts, which is worth mentioning even when it's not the reason you called.

What Affects The Price

Repair pricing depends on which component has failed, and whether the issue turns out to be repairable or points to full replacement instead.

Component involved

A thermostat or element repair costs less than a tank that's actually leaking from the body.

Gas vs. electric

Gas units have additional components (thermocouple, gas valve) that electric units don't.

Unit age

A unit near the end of its expected service life may be better replaced than repaired, which we'll tell you honestly.
