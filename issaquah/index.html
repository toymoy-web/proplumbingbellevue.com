Coverage & Local Notes

Two Issaquahs: Olde Town And The Highlands

Issaquah was incorporated in 1892, and its historic core, Olde Town and the Gilman neighborhood, sits close to the original downtown, with housing stock considerably older than the rest of the city. Squak Mountain rises directly behind it, and the mature tree cover on its lower slopes creates the same kind of root intrusion pattern we see in Bellevue's older neighborhoods.

The Issaquah Highlands and Talus, both up in the newer 98029 ZIP, are master-planned communities built mostly during the 2000s and 2010s on a ridge above the valley, with modern PEX and copper plumbing throughout and none of the aging-pipe issues Olde Town deals with.

Issaquah is a founding member of Cascade Water Alliance, the same regional water supplier used across Bellevue and most of this service area.

Issaquah is also home to a well-known salmon run each fall, drawing residents to Issaquah Creek near downtown, a real point of local identity. Olde Town's proximity to the creek and mature streamside vegetation is part of why root intrusion shows up so consistently in that part of the city.

Issaquah also sits at a genuine geographic crossroads, at the base of Tiger, Squak, and Cougar Mountains, with I-90 as the primary route connecting it to both Seattle and the rest of the Eastside. That keeps our dispatch times reasonable even for the more remote hillside properties.

What Issaquah Homeowners Call About Most

Hydro Jetting In Olde Town, Tankless Service In The Highlands

Olde Town and Gilman generate a real, recurring stream of drain and sewer calls tied to root intrusion from Squak Mountain's tree cover, often needing hydro jetting rather than a simple snake. Up in the Issaquah Highlands, the calls run newer: tankless water heater descaling and fixture work in homes built within the last two decades.

Talus, sitting at the base of Cougar Mountain, tends to follow the Highlands pattern rather than Olde Town's. With roughly 40,000 residents, Issaquah is smaller than Bellevue or Redmond, but the split between its historic core and newer ridge development gives us two genuinely different plumbing profiles to work from on any given day.

Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for Issaquah, the same as every city we serve from our Bellevue base.
