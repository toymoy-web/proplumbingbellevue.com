Typical VisitSame-Day

A slow kitchen sink is usually the first sign of a clog. A backed-up main line is usually the last, and the two need different tools. We clear both, starting with the least invasive method that actually solves the problem instead of jumping straight to the most expensive one.

Most single-fixture clogs in Bellevue homes come down to grease, soap buildup, or hair, and a cable snake clears those in one visit. Recurring clogs on a main line are a different story. Mature trees are common across Eastside neighborhoods like Lake Hills and Wilburton, and root intrusion into an aging clay or cast-iron sewer line is a real, recurring cause of a drain that clogs again a few weeks after snaking.

When a clog keeps coming back, we run a camera through the line before recommending anything further. That tells us whether it's root intrusion, a bellied section of pipe, or something else, instead of guessing and charging for another snake visit that won't hold.

For kitchens specifically, we also look at habits that contribute to recurring grease buildup, since a drain that's cleared today but fed the same way tomorrow tends to clog again on a predictable schedule. A quick conversation about what's actually going down the drain often does more for preventing the next call than the cleaning itself.

For homes with a history of repeated main-line clogs, we also offer scheduled maintenance cleaning on a set interval rather than waiting for the next backup. For a property with known root intrusion, this tends to work out cheaper and considerably less disruptive than emergency calls timed around whenever the line happens to fail next. It's worth discussing once a property has needed more than one emergency clearing in a short span.

Kitchen Drains

Kitchen Sink Clogs

Kitchen drains clog from grease, food and soap that cool and harden inside the pipe. Most of the kitchen clogs we get called for start as a slow drain days before the sink stops draining.

A cable usually clears the blockage near the sink. If the sink backs up again within weeks, grease further down the line is the likely cause and hydro jetting is the better fix. We avoid chemical cleaners because they can harm older pipe.

Method

Snaking Versus Hydro Jetting

A cable, also called a snake, breaks through a clog or pulls it out. It is the right tool for a single fixture and for most first-time blockages.

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour grease, scale and roots off the pipe wall. It suits recurring clogs and main lines as long as the pipe is sound enough to take it. Our hydro jetting page explains when we recommend it.

Bathrooms

Shower And Tub Drain Clogs

Hair and soap are the usual cause when a shower or tub drains slowly. We clear the blockage and check the trap, and if several fixtures are slow at once we look at the main line instead.

Main Line

Main Line Backups And Cleanouts

When every drain in the house is slow, or the lowest tub or a floor drain backs up first, the blockage is usually in the main line. Those backups are urgent because wastewater has nowhere else to go.

Under Bellevue city code, the side sewer on private property belongs to the property owner. Anyone cleaning an existing side sewer must notify the utility first, and the owner can be liable for damages if debris causes a blockage downstream. We clear lines with that in mind.
