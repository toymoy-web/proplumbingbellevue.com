The Neighborhood

Plumbing In Newport Hills, Southwest Bellevue

Newport Hills sits in the southwest corner of Bellevue. The City of Bellevue's 2025 Newport existing-conditions report describes the wider Newport planning area as bounded by Lake Washington on the west, Newcastle on the south, Factoria and Somerset on the east, and I-90 on the north, with I-405 running through the middle. The report counts 3,839 housing units there: 3,289 single-family homes (about 86%) and 550 multifamily units (about 14%).

According to the same report, most of the low-density housing was built in the 1960s and 1970s, and the Newport Hills subdivisions were platted in 1958. Smaller named pockets include Lake Heights, Greenwich Crest and Pembrook Meadows, which were generally built as units with limited arterial access. The city designates Newport Hills Shopping Center, at 119th Ave SE and SE 56th St, as a Neighborhood Center: a small commercial area in an otherwise residential setting.

Newport Shores, the canal-side community west of I-405, belongs to the same planning area. The report notes that it is almost entirely flat because it was built on fill after Lake Washington was lowered in 1916. Green space is a big part of the picture too: Coal Creek Natural Area runs about three miles, Newport Hills Park sits at 120th Ave SE and SE 60th St, and tree canopy covers 43% of the neighborhood compared with 40% citywide.

Plumbing Services

What Plumbing Help Looks Like Here

A planning report can tell us when homes were built, but it cannot tell us what is inside a particular wall. Houses from the 1960s and 1970s may have their original plumbing, partial updates or a full repipe, and the only way to know is to look. A plumber can check supply line material, shutoff valves and the sewer lateral without assuming anything in advance.

Water Heater Installation & Replacement

Homes with several bathrooms and a larger household put steady demand on a tank. If yours is past its 10 to 12 year typical service life, replacement planning beats waiting for a failure.

Sewer Line Repair

Drain Cleaning

Leak Detection

Good To Know

Low Ground And Drainage Questions

Properties on flat, low ground, such as the canal-side streets of Newport Shores, are sometimes asked about crawlspace moisture or sump pumps. That is general plumbing information, not a statement that any home in the area has a problem. If you notice standing water or a persistent damp smell, a sump pump assessment is a reasonable first step.

Before You Call

If A Water Heater Starts Leaking

  • Turn off the cold water supply valve on the pipe above the tank.
  • Switch off power to an electric heater at the breaker, or set a gas heater's control to off.
  • Put a bucket under the leak and move stored items away from the floor around the tank.
  • Take a photo of the tank label and the leak, then call so the plumber knows the model.
