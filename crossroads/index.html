The Neighborhood

Plumbing In Crossroads, East Bellevue

Crossroads is a busy east Bellevue neighborhood built around Crossroads Shopping Center. The City of Bellevue's 2025 Crossroads existing-conditions report puts its boundaries roughly at NE Bel-Red Road and Northup Way on the north, 164th Ave NE on the east, NE 8th St on the south, and 136th Ave NE and Glendale Country Club on the west. That is about 1.3 square miles, bordering Wilburton, BelRed, Northeast Bellevue and Lake Hills, with a short border with Redmond along NE 20th St.

The housing mix here is unusual for Bellevue. The report counts 996 parcels, of which 758 are low-density single-family (about 76%) and 144 are multifamily (about 14%). Even so, about 82% of residents live in multifamily buildings, because apartment and condo complexes house far more people per parcel. The single-family streets mostly end in cul-de-sacs, and named pockets include Glendale, Covington Park, Rock Creek, Chevy Chase and Fox Glen.

The report places the mall's opening at 1962, complete by 1964, with annexation into Bellevue beginning that year. Chevy Chase and neighboring subdivisions were built out in the 1950s or early 1960s. Crossroads Park, at more than 34 acres, and the Bellevue Aquatic Center beside Odle Middle School are the area's main public facilities.

Plumbing Services

Two Kinds Of Plumbing Calls In One Neighborhood

Because so many Crossroads residents rent or own in multifamily buildings, plumbing problems here often involve more than one household. A leak in one unit can reach the unit below, and a shutoff may be controlled by building management or an HOA rather than by you. Single-family streets are the other half of the picture, where the owner handles everything from the main shutoff to the sewer lateral.

Leak Detection

Water showing up on a ceiling or wall with no visible source is a classic multi-unit problem. Tracing it early limits damage to neighbors.

Water Damage Restoration

When a leak has already soaked drywall or flooring, drying and repairs are coordinated with the plumbing fix.

Faucet & Sink Repair

Dripping faucets, failed shutoff valves and worn fixtures are routine repairs in both apartments and houses.

Toilet Repair & Installation

Running or leaking toilets waste water quickly and are usually a straightforward repair or replacement.

Good To Know

Renters, Owners And Building Managers

If you rent, report a leak to your landlord or property manager first, since they usually control the building's shutoff valves and repair decisions. If you own a condo, check your HOA rules about who is responsible for pipes inside the walls and who is responsible for shared risers.

Single-family owners in the Chevy Chase, Glendale and Fox Glen areas can call directly. Whatever the building type, it helps to know where your unit's water shutoff is before you ever need it.

Before You Call

If Water Is Coming Through A Ceiling

  • Shut off the water at your unit's valve if you can find it, or at the main if you own the house.
  • Turn off power to the affected room at the breaker if water is near lights or outlets.
  • Contact building management or your HOA if neighbors could be affected.
  • Photograph the damage for your insurer, then call to have the source located.
