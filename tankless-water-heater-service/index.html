ServiceDescale & Flush

Tankless water heaters need periodic descaling in a way tank units don't, because mineral scale builds up directly on the heat exchanger and reduces efficiency over time. Manufacturers generally recommend a flush every 12 to 18 months, more often in homes without a water softener.

We service tankless units from all the major manufacturers stocked in this market. When a unit is showing an error code, we start there, since the code usually narrows down whether it's a scale issue, a venting problem, or a component failure before we open anything up. That first read often saves an unnecessary full teardown.

Homeowners converting from a tank system to tankless for the first time often underestimate the venting requirements. Tankless units need specific venting different from a standard tank, and retrofitting an older home sometimes means running new venting through a wall or roof rather than reusing what's already there, which is worth knowing before you commit to a conversion.

Gas supply is another thing worth checking on a tankless install or service call. Tankless units draw significantly more gas at peak demand than a standard tank heater, and an older home's existing gas line isn't always sized to handle that draw without a supply upgrade. This matters most on a first-time tank-to-tankless conversion, but it's worth confirming during any tankless work.

When A Tankless Unit Needs Service, Not Replacement

A common call is a tankless system that's a few years old and either isn't heating as consistently as it used to, or is throwing an error code the homeowner doesn't recognize. Both are frequently a maintenance issue, mineral scale buildup inside the heat exchanger, rather than a sign the unit is failing outright.

Local Neighborhoods

Tankless Units In Downtown And BelRed

Newer buildings in Downtown, BelRed, and the Spring District lean on tankless water heaters, often in tight mechanical closets. Townhomes and condos in Factoria and Eastgate that use the same equipment have the same service needs. Those are an annual flush of the heat exchanger, a clear condensate drain, and clean venting, so they are worth scheduling before a winter cold snap.

What Callers Ask About

Tankless Flushing And Descaling

Flushing is the most requested tankless service we get. A descaling flush circulates a cleaning solution through the heat exchanger to dissolve mineral scale, which restores flow and heating performance.

Most calls start with an error code, lukewarm water or a drop in flow. A flush often fixes it, and we read the code first to confirm that scale is really the cause.
