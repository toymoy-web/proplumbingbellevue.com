First StepCamera Inspection

We don't recommend a sewer line repair without first confirming what's actually wrong with a camera. A recurring clog can mean root intrusion, a bellied or sagging section of pipe, or a cracked joint, and each of those has a different fix, so guessing costs more in the long run than a proper inspection up front.

Root intrusion is the most common cause we find in Bellevue's older neighborhoods, where mature trees planted decades ago now sit directly over clay or cast-iron sewer lines. Roots enter through pipe joints seeking moisture. Once they're established, the line needs either localized spot repair or, for more extensive damage, a full replacement of the affected section, done to Uniform Plumbing Code standards for pipe material and slope.

The repair method also depends on what's under the affected section. A line running under a driveway or a mature tree's root system is a different scope than one running through open yard. We factor that into both the camera inspection and the repair plan, since access constraints can shift a job from a half-day repair to a multi-day one.

For repairs under an existing structure, like a line running beneath a finished basement, we plan the access point to minimize how much flooring or slab needs to be opened, sometimes working from an exterior access point instead of cutting through a finished interior space. This adds planning time upfront but generally saves both cost and disruption compared to a straightforward but more invasive excavation path, and it's a conversation we have with you before any cutting starts, not after.

Emergency Response

Emergency Sewer Backup And Sewer Line Failure

A sewer backup is a health hazard and one of the most urgent calls we take. Wastewater coming up through floor drains, tubs or toilets means the line is blocked or broken somewhere between the house and the main.

Stop using water, keep everyone out of the affected area, and call our emergency line. We clear the immediate blockage, run a camera to find the cause, and explain whether a repair is needed or cleaning will hold.

Local Neighborhoods

Sewer Lines Near Greenbelts And Mature Trees

Sewer laterals in Lake Hills, Crossroads, and Newport Hills can be 60 to 70 years old, and roots from mature trees along the Lake Hills Greenbelt and the Coal Creek greenbelt look for weak joints. In Bridle Trails, larger wooded lots mean longer laterals under more trees. A camera inspection shows whether the pipe needs a spot repair or a full replacement before anyone digs.

Pricing

What Affects The Price

Sewer repair pricing depends entirely on what the camera actually finds: a localized spot repair costs significantly less than replacing a full section of damaged line.

Extent of damage

A single root intrusion point is a smaller repair than a line with damage along its full length.

Depth and access

A shallow line is faster to access than one running deep under a driveway or foundation.

Repair method

Trenchless spot repair is sometimes possible for localized damage, which avoids full excavation.

Responsibility

Who Is Responsible For The Sewer Line

Under Bellevue city code, a side sewer on private property belongs to the property owner, who is responsible for developing, maintaining and repairing it. The city owns the sewer facilities in the public right-of-way.

That is why a camera inspection matters. It shows whether the problem is on your side of the line, and it gives you a clear picture to work from if the defect turns out to be closer to the main.
