The Neighborhood

Plumbing In Eastgate, Bellevue

Eastgate is a mixed residential and employment area along I-90. The City of Bellevue's January 2026 Eastgate existing-conditions report describes its boundaries as 137th Ave SE on the west, SE 23rd St on the north, 168th Ave SE on the east and SE 41st St on the south, about 1,760 acres in all. The report notes that a 2012 annexation brought the full area into Bellevue.

The report counts 3,302 housing units: 2,301 single-family (70%) and 1,001 multifamily (30%), typically no more than three stories. Named residential pockets include Madrona Park, mostly condominiums in the northwest, Horizon Heights, mostly single-family in the south, and Cougar Hills, single-family in the southeast. Housing dates mainly from the mid-20th century and the 1980s and 1990s.

Eastgate is also a work destination. The report cites about 331 acres of office land, Bellevue College on roughly 100 acres with enrollment above 20,000, and retail at Eastgate Plaza and Sunset Village. Parks include Sunset Park, Lattawood Park and Spiritridge Park.

Homes, Condos And Commercial Spaces

Eastgate calls come from three directions: owners of single-family homes, condo residents in buildings like those in Madrona Park, and small businesses in the commercial and office areas. Each needs a different conversation, from who controls the shutoff to whether the work needs a permit or scheduling around business hours.

Water Heater Repair

Hot water that runs out quickly, leaks at the base of the tank or strange noises are the usual reasons for a repair call.

Leak Detection

Commercial Plumbing

Restaurants, offices and retail tenants need plumbing work scheduled around operations.

Backflow Testing & Prevention

Good To Know

Working Around Buildings And Business Hours

For condo and multi-tenant buildings, we recommend contacting building management before any water shutoff that could affect other units. For businesses, tell us your hours when you call so work can be planned to limit downtime.

Homeowners in Horizon Heights and Cougar Hills deal with the same questions as other single-family neighborhoods: water heater age, drain performance and what is behind the walls. See drain cleaning if a clog keeps coming back.

Owners and tenants alike benefit from a few questions before any visit: who authorizes the work, whether a permit might be needed, and whether other units or tenants share the line. Settling these up front avoids delays, and a short written summary of the diagnosis helps when you need approval from a landlord, HOA or property manager. Keep photos of any visible damage and your own notes on when the problem started.

Before You Call

If A Business Loses Water Or Has A Backup

  • Identify whether the problem affects one fixture, one restroom or the whole space.
  • Stop using affected fixtures to avoid spreading a backup.
  • Notify your landlord or property manager if the building's shared lines may be involved.
  • Call with your suite number, hours and a short description of the problem.
