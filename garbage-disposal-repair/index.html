Garbage Disposal Repair & Installation in Bellevue, WA

Jammed, leaking, or dead disposals repaired or replaced, usually in the same visit.

A disposal that hums but won't spin is almost always a jam, and that's often fixable on the spot without a replacement. A disposal that's silent and unresponsive, or one that's leaking from the bottom, is more likely at the end of its life and worth replacing rather than repairing.

We check both possibilities before recommending anything. If it's a jam, we clear it and test under load before we consider the job done. If it's leaking, we check whether it's the disposal body itself or just a loose connection to the drain line or dishwasher hose, since those are a much simpler fix than a full unit replacement. Either way, you get a straight answer before any parts are ordered.

Builder-grade disposals installed in Bellevue condos and townhomes during original construction are often a lower horsepower unit than what a household actually needs, which shows up as more frequent jamming over time rather than a single failure. When we're already replacing a unit, we'll flag if a higher-horsepower model would genuinely reduce how often this becomes a recurring call.

When a disposal replacement is needed, we also check the electrical connection and switch. A disposal that seems dead is occasionally an electrical issue rather than a disposal failure, particularly in older homes where the switch or outlet hasn't been updated in decades. Confirming this before quoting a replacement avoids selling a new unit when the actual fix is a simpler electrical repair.
