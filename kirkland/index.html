Coverage & Local Notes

Moss Bay, Bridle Trails, And Totem Lake

Downtown Kirkland, centered on Moss Bay and the Lake Washington waterfront, is the oldest part of the city, founded in 1888 and incorporated in 1905, making it one of the oldest cities we cover beyond Bellevue itself. Homes near the water and in the adjacent Norkirk neighborhood often date back many decades.

Bridle Trails, a heavily wooded residential neighborhood built around Bridle Trails State Park, has an older tree canopy that makes root intrusion in main sewer lines a real, recurring issue, similar to what we see in parts of Bellevue. Juanita and Totem Lake to the north have a mix of older lakeside homes and newer mixed-use development.

Kirkland is a founding member of Cascade Water Alliance, the same regional water supplier as Bellevue, so water hardness and treatment are consistent across both cities.

Kirkland's roughly 4.86 square miles of water frontage along Lake Washington also means some lakeside and low-lying properties deal with a higher water table than the rest of the city. That's a real factor worth checking when sizing a sump pump or planning drainage work near the shoreline.

At over 95,000 residents, Kirkland is the second-largest city we cover outside Bellevue itself, behind only Renton. That means more of nearly everything: more older housing stock to service, more waterfront properties, and a wider range of construction eras within a single neighborhood than smaller cities like Newcastle see.

What Kirkland Homeowners Call About Most

Leak Detection In The Waterfront Core, Root Intrusion In Bridle Trails

Downtown and waterfront Kirkland calls skew toward leak detection, both because of the older housing stock and because homes closer to the lake deal with a higher water table. Bridle Trails and other tree-lined established neighborhoods generate a steady stream of sewer line calls tied to root intrusion from mature trees planted decades ago. Juanita and Totem Lake, further north, run somewhere in between the two patterns, and every repair we complete follows Uniform Plumbing Code standards regardless of which pattern applies. Median home values in Kirkland now run well above a million dollars, and even at that price point, original plumbing from the home's construction era is common in the older waterfront blocks.

Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for Kirkland, the same as every city we serve from our Bellevue base.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Kirkland

  • Turn off electricity to the affected area if water is near outlets or fixtures.
